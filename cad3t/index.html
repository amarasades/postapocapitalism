Cad3t

CAD // NURBS modelling

Full CAD, from the cabinet to the circuit board inside it, straight to the CNC.

Cad3t is a CAD app for Windows, built on its own NURBS geometry kernel. Model furniture, enclosures, shop fixtures and the spare part a broken machine is missing. Send exact geometry to other CAD software, meshes to engines, and drawings and cut lists to the workshop.

Also written CAD3T, said "cadet". Part of the postapocapitalism software family, with Sc3ne and R3mesher.

What it does

06 functions
01 // Surfaces

Curves and surfaces, exactly

Lines to G2 blends, helices and offsets; extrude, revolve, loft, sweep, edge surfaces. Fill spans any closed loop of curves with the smoothest single surface that fits, carrying on its neighbours' direction across the boundary.

02 // Solids

Real solids

Union, difference and intersection on any closed solids, flat or curved. Fillet outside edges and inside corners, split and trim with curves or surfaces, measure length, area and volume, check continuity with zebra stripes and curvature.

03 // Input

Type it or click it

Start typing a command anywhere and the command line suggests it. Place points by clicking with snaps, or by typing coordinates, relative moves, angles and arithmetic: @40<30, (100+20)/2.

04 // Workshop

Ready to be made

Dimensioned shop drawings with hidden lines (PDF, SVG, DXF). A cut list with sizes, materials, quantities and cost (CSV). STL for printing, STEP and IGES for CNC and other CAD. Standard screws, bolts, nuts and brackets to click into place.

05 // Fixtures

Furniture that resizes

Shelving, wall shelves, display tables, rails, plinths, counters and framed panels: change a size and the parts rebuild in place. Turn your own model into a template, then send it to Sc3ne to furnish a store.

06 // Materials

Materials and UVs

A PBR material library (wood, metals, plastics, glass), cut a face and assign it its own. Unwrap and pack UVs with seams on the model's own edges, UDIM tiles and lightmap UVs. Export to glTF and OBJ with every texture.

How it works

Draw // build // check // make
  1. DrawCurves on a construction plane that follows the view. Or bring a mesh in: STL, OBJ and glTF come in through Mesh to CAD as editable faces.
  2. BuildSurfaces and solids, booleans, fillets, trims. Every step is undoable and the model saves a recovery copy.
  3. CheckZebra, curvature, continuity, volume. Render a quick still to see it in materials.
  4. MakeShop drawing and cut list to the workshop, STEP or IGES to the CNC, STL to the printer, glTF to the engine.

Questions

What kind of CAD is Cad3t?

NURBS surface and solid modelling, the kind used for furniture, product design and enclosures, with its own geometry kernel. Geometry stays exact until you export a mesh.

Will it open my files from other CAD software?

STEP, IGES and DXF come straight in, and STEP and IGES go back out exact, for Rhino, SolidWorks, Fusion, Inventor, FreeCAD and Alias.

Can I try it?

Not yet. Cad3t is in beta and not publicly downloadable.

How do you spell it?

Cad3t, with a 3 for the e, said "cadet". CAD3T in capitals is the same app.
